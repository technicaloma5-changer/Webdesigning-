CSS Text Alignment

CSS text alignment properties help us control the horizontal and vertical positioning of text.

1. Text Align Left

This paragraph is aligned to the left side. Left alignment is commonly used for normal website content and paragraphs.

2. Text Align Right

This paragraph is aligned to the right side. Right alignment is useful in some special design layouts.

3. Text Align Center

This paragraph is aligned in the center. Center alignment is commonly used for headings.

4. Text Align Justify

Justify alignment adjusts the spacing between words so that the text lines extend evenly across the available width. It is often used in articles, blogs and other long-form content.
